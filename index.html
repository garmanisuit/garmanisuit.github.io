A suit on a short Bangkok trip: what fits in your days

At G.Armani Suit a suit typically takes 3 to 5 days over two to three fittings. Shirts, trousers and alterations need far less time. This guide sets each garment against the days you have.

Which garments fit into 24 to 48 hours?

Shirts, a single pair of trousers and most alterations fit into one or two days.

A shirt takes 24 to 48 hours, sometimes the same day. One pair of trousers is ready the same day or the next. The shop finishes most alterations the same day, and the mall's shops can fill the wait. Times vary with the garment and how busy the workroom is that day.

How long does a suit take?

A two-piece suit typically takes 3 to 5 days, and that window runs from cutting to finishing.

Time for corrections between fittings, not sewing speed, usually sets the pace. A cloth the shop already holds typically saves a day compared with ordering one in, so share your dates before choosing.

Larger orders, such as a three-piece suit, a dinner suit or a wedding party, should be started on the day you land.

What should you do early in your trip?

Message the shop your dates before you travel, and visit within your first two days.

Starting early usually buys an extra fitting. Bring a garment you own that fits well, and know what the suit is for before you choose cloth. Before you leave the shop, book the second fitting.

What if your flight moves?

Contact the shop as soon as your dates change.

The stage the garment has reached decides the options. Late changes may mean the last steps are brought forward, or that the garment is finished after you leave and sent on. The pattern stays on file, and a later order starts with confirming a couple of measurements.

When is express the wrong answer?

Express is wrong whenever it replaces the fitting.

A suit made with no fitting is an off-the-peg suit, and the shop says it should be described as one. The shop suggests ordering shirts first and the suit on a later trip when the window is too short.
